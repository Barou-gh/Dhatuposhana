Pallor of skin and mucous membranes, cold limbs, roughness, vein collapse, fatigue, and craving for acidic/sour foods.

  • • Pale skin, lips, and nail beds (Panduta)
  • • Collapse / flatness of blood vessels (Sirasithilata)

  • • Craving for sour & acidic tastes (Amla-prarthana)

Rakta Vriddhi (Blood Excess / Hyperemia)

Redness of eyes & skin, burning sensation, hypertension, skin disorders (Visarpa, Kustha), and hemorrhagic tendencies.

  • • Redness of eyes and skin (Raktaksheta / Raktangata)
  • • Burning sensation in body (Daha)
  • • Bleeding tendencies & hemoptysis (Raktapitta)
  • • Inflammatory skin lesions & gout (Vatarakta)
