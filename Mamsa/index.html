Mamsa Dhatu - Medical & Ayurvedic Science

1. What is Mamsa Dhatu?

In Ayurveda, Mamsa Dhatu represents the muscular system—the third fundamental tissue of the human body. Formed from Rakta Dhatu (blood tissue) through the metabolic action of Mamsagni, Mamsa Dhatu provides physical strength, structural bulk, posture, and protective coverage over the skeletal frame (Asthi Dhatu).

2. Anatomical Composition & Realism

This 3D medical visualization models the volumetric, three-dimensional muscular anatomy with high fidelity:

  • Volumetric Muscle Groups: Distinct superficial and deep muscle layers attached to bone origin/insertion points.
  • Striated Fiber Orientation: Realistic biological fiber alignment converging into glistening tendons (Snayu).
  • Connective Tissue & Fascia: Pale cream tendons and subtle translucent fascia binding muscle belly bundles (Peshana).

3. Clinical & Physiological Functions (Karma)

Lepana (Body Plastering): The muscle tissue envelops organs and bones, shielding vital centers (Marma) and providing contour.

Bala (Physical Strength): Optimal Mamsa Dhatu yields endurance, physical resistance, and joint stability.
