Reproductive System

Testes (Vrishana)

वृषण (Vrishana)
Primary Sex Organ Scrotal Sac / Pelvic Origin

3D Anatomical Architecture

Pair of ovoid glandular organs enclosed within the scrotum. Surrounded by tunica vaginalis and tunica albuginea. Contains 200–300 lobules housing tightly coiled seminiferous tubules responsible for spermatogenesis.

Shukra Dhatu Integration

Primary locus of Shukra Dhatu production (Shukra-Vaha Srotas Mula). Transforms precursor Dhatus (Majja & Rasa) into refined reproductive seeds and vital hormones.

Primary Secretion Sperm Cells & Testosterone

Physical Quality Viscous, Ivory-Pearlescent
Associated Nerve/Vessel Testicular Artery & Pampiniform Plexus

  • Efferent Ductules → Head of Epididymis

Fundamental Concept

What is Shukra Dhatu?

In Ayurvedic Physiology (Sharira Kriya), Shukra Dhatu is the seventh and final tissue layer formed through the metabolic transformation chain (Dhatu Parinama):

Rasa → Rakta → Mamsa → Meda → Asthi → Majja → SHUKRA

It represents the essence of all organs, controlling reproduction, vitality, courage, aura, and ultimate immunity (Ojas).

Qualities of Pure Shukra (Shuddha Shukra Lakshana)

"Sphatikabham Dravam Snigdham Madhuram Madhugandhi Cha |
Shukram Ichanti Eke Shuddham Thailakshaudranibham Tathaa ||" — Charaka Samhita, Chikitsa Sthana
  • Sphatika-sankasha: Clear, crystalline, pearlescent white glow.
  • Snigdha & Dravam: Smooth, unctuous, fluidly viscous.
  • Madhura: Sweet in metabolic taste and post-digestive outcome.
  • Madhugandhi: Characteristic natural aroma.
  • Bahula: Abundant, dense in reproductive nutrients.

Component Anatomical Equivalent

Shukra Kshaya (Deficiency Symptoms)

  • General fatigue, physical weakness, loss of muscular stamina.
  • Dryness of mouth, joint stiffness, reduced vitality and drive.
  • Oligospermia, low testosterone signs, erectile insufficiency.
  • Premature aging signs due to diminished Ojas generation.

Shukra Vriddhi (Excess & Congestion)

  • Hyper-secretion of seminal fluid, prostatic fullness.
  • Increased libido without emotional balance, pelvic heaviness.
  • Risk of prostatic hypertrophy or seminal vesicle distension.

These herbs act directly on the Shukra-Agni (tissue fire) to enhance spermatogenesis, nourish seminal fluid, and fortify pelvic vascular flow.
