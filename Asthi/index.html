5TH DHATU

Asthi Dhatu (Bone Tissue)

In Ayurvedic Physiology, Asthi Dhatu represents the solid structural scaffolding of the body. It provides rigidity, stability, protects vital visceral organs (Prana Ayatana), and supports muscular attachments (Mamsa Dhatu).

🔑 Key Ayurvedic Attributes

  • Mahabhuta Dominance: Prithvi (Earth - solidity) & Vayu (Air - porosity/cavities).
  • Upadhatu (Secondary Tissues): Danta (Teeth).
  • Mala (Excretory Products): Nakha (Nails) & Kesha/Loma (Hair).
  • Asthivaha Srotas: Channels originating from Medo Dhatu (adipose tissue) & Jaghana (pelvic region).
  • Associated Dhatu: Majja Dhatu (Bone Marrow), contained directly within Asthi cavities.

🌿 Functional Purpose (Karma)

"Dharanam Asthnam Karma" — The fundamental function of Asthi Dhatu is Dharana (supporting and sustaining the body's posture, weight, and internal organs against gravity).

SKELETAL SYSTEM

Human Adult Skeleton

Comprising 206 distinct articulated bones, the skeleton forms the axial and appendicular framework of the human body, providing cortical protection, leverage for locomotion, and hematopoiesis.

🔬 Histological & Material Composition

  • Cortical (Compact) Bone: Dense outer ivory shell giving mechanical strength & rigidity.
  • Trabecular (Cancellous) Bone: Porous internal honeycombed lattice distributing load forces.
  • Bone Matrix: 65% Inorganic mineral hydroxyapatite [Ca10(PO4)6(OH)2], 35% Organic Type I Collagen fibres.
  • Articular Cartilage: Smooth hyaline cartilage covering joint articulation heads.
